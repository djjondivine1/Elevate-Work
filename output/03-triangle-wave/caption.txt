SOFTER THAN A SQUARE WAVE. Triangle Wave, explained.

A triangle wave rises and falls in straight lines. Its harmonics fade out fast, so it sounds smooth and mellow instead of harsh.

Do this tonight: Reach for it when you want body without bite.
