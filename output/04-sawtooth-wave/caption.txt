THE WAVE BEHIND BIG EDM SOUNDS. Sawtooth Wave, explained.

A sawtooth ramps up, then drops sharply. It carries every harmonic, even and odd. That's why it sounds so full and so bright.

Do this tonight: Bright, thick sounds need space. Don't stack two on one beat.
