ONE KNOB. A WHOLE NEW SOUND. Pulse Wave, explained.

A pulse wave is a square wave with an adjustable width. That width is called the duty cycle. Change it and the tone changes.

Do this tonight: Sweep the pulse width slowly and listen to the tone change.
