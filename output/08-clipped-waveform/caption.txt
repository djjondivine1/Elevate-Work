FLAT TOPS MEAN BAD SOUND. Clipped Waveform, explained.

Clipping happens when a signal is pushed past the maximum level. The peaks get cut flat, and that adds harsh distortion you can't cleanly undo.

Do this tonight: Keep every meter out of the red. Fix loudness at the amp.
