FIND THE KICK. FIND THE MIX.. Kick Drum Waveform, explained.

A kick is a short, heavy thump. It starts with a big peak, then fades fast. It carries the most low-end energy in the track.

Do this tonight: Cut the low end on the incoming track. Swap bass on the bar line.
