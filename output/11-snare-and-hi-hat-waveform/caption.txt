THE SNARE TELLS YOU WHERE YOU ARE. Snare & Hi-Hat Waveform, explained.

Between the kicks sit the snare and the hi-hat. They're thinner and brighter, and they tell you exactly where you are in the groove.

Do this tonight: Find 2 and 4 by the snare. Count to the next fill.
