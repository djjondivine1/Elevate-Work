CUT IT CLEAN. NO BLEND. NO FEAR.. Quick Cut Transition, explained.

A long blend can kill the punch of a drop. A quick cut switches tracks instantly, on the beat, with no overlap and no effects. Done right, it sounds sharp and on purpose.

Do this tonight: Cut on the first beat of a new phrase and the crowd will feel it land.
