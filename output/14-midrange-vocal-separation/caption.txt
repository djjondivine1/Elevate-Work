MAKE ROOM FOR THE VOCALS. Midrange Vocal Separation, explained.

Vocals, guitars, synths and snares all fight for the same midrange. When two tracks pile up there, the vocal gets buried and the mix sounds muddy.

Do this tonight: Cut the competing mids on the other track before you boost the vocal.
