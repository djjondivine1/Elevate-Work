THE KNOB THAT MAKES FILTERS SING. Resonance Control, explained.

A plain filter sweep just takes sound away. Resonance boosts the frequencies right at the cutoff, so the sweep gets a clear peak you can hear and feel.

Do this tonight: Raise resonance slowly while you sweep the cutoff to build tension.
