EQ + FILTER: THE CLEAN MIX COMBO. EQ and Filter Combo, explained.

When two full tracks play together, their lows and highs pile up and the mix turns messy. EQ shapes each range. A filter removes everything above or below a point. Together they keep it clean.

Do this tonight: Close a low-pass on the old track while a high-pass opens on the new one.
