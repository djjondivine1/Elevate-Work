YOUR PADS ARE A MAP. Hot Cue Map, explained.

Cues placed on whatever sounds interesting, with the grid slightly off, make every jump sloppy. Too many cues is as bad as none.

Do this tonight: Set four cues on five tracks, then mix using only cue jumps.
