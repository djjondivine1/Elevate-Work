MAKE A TRACK DISSOLVE. Echo-Out, explained.

Some tracks have no outro, or you simply need out. A hard stop sounds like a mistake, and a late echo leaves the dry track sitting on top of its own tail.

Do this tonight: Echo-out three tracks in a row, each into a clean start on the next phrase.
