LONG BLEND OR SHORT?. Long or Short Blend, explained.

A long blend of two competing vocal hooks sounds chaotic, however well the beats lock. Blend length is a decision, not a habit.

Do this tonight: Mix the same two tracks three ways and keep the version the pair survives.
